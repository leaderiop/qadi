The dock applies every input whatever a host listener on any of its stores throws, on `configure` and on each record the timeline accepts (a throw a listener makes on a record still ends the read that handed it over, and the timeline status says `Stopped`): the timeline, the sightings rail, the roles, the simulation's ports, the gates, the diagnostics and the shown reading are each applied, and what the listeners threw is rethrown after the last step, one value as itself and several as one `AggregateError` in step order, where a step whose own listeners threw more than once contributes one nested `AggregateError`, never flattened. A listener that throws on a status that announces work no longer keeps the work from starting: the timeline read, the diagnostics poll and the simulation run start, and the throw is rethrown once they have. The timeline feed's `configure` with a new `capacity` rethrows a timeline listener's throw and a status listener's throw on the `Reading` as one `AggregateError`, the timeline's first, where a status throw used to replace the timeline listener's and leave no read started. No export changes.
