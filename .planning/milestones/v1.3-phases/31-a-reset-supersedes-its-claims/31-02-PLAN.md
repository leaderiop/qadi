## Flagged assumptions

- **SUPR-02 (probe: unclassified).** "`clear` is reduced to the steps the adapter still owns" is read as D-07 states it: one
  `Effect.sync` holding exactly three statements (empty `entries`, empty `insertionOrder`, `shared.reset()`), with FIFO
  eviction, the capacity bookkeeping and the successes-only policy unchanged in the hook. Task 2's second verify checks the
  three statements in order.
- **Transition tables instead of literal assignments.** D-01 says onExit settles only when `standing === "Live"`; that guard
  stays literal. The two transitions D-02 names are written as module-scope tables, not as `entry.standing = "…"`
  assignments, because the only reader of `standing` (that guard) cannot tell Superseded from Abandoned by design: each
  literal assignment would be a Stryker mutant no test can kill, and three of them would put `SharedComputation.ts` below its
  D-12 base. D-12 asks for such equivalents to be restructured away. The tables state D-02 once and exhaustively, their
  value type `Exclude<Standing, "Live">` makes a return to Live a compile error, and every row with an observable effect is
  pinned by the races. The one row with none, a Superseded claim's last leave keeping it Superseded, is the distinction
  SUPR-01 asks for as a state, not as a behaviour; Task 1's SUMMARY says so. Reversible: a later reader can inline them.
