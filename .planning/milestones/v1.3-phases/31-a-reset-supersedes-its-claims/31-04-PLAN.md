1. ADR-QD-132 (D-13): append `## Amendment (2026-11-12, CCR-QD-422, ARCH-79)` at the end. Say: a reset is a second way a claim stops being current. Before, `reset` only forgot the in-flight table, so a forgotten claim that somebody still awaited settled into the adapter, and `DecisionCache` closed that with a counter of its own, read when a claim was made and compared when it settled. Now a claim has one closed standing, Live, Superseded or Abandoned; `reset` supersedes every in-flight claim in the same synchronous step that empties the table; only a Live claim's exit reaches the adapter, through a `settle(key, exit)` hook that replaces the hook that returned a closure when the claim was made. A superseded claim is treated as decisions 1 and 2 treat an abandoned one: it is not joinable, its exit is dropped, it still answers every waiter, and it is interrupted when its last waiter leaves. `DecisionCache` keeps no counter; `clear` empties its entries and insertion order and calls `reset`. Decision 3 still holds: the interface, the metric order and `DecisionCache.test.ts` are unchanged. ADR-QD-144, reserved for this, is unused, because this is the same decision applied to a second cause. Link BEH-QD-491 and INV-QD-307 with their slugs.
