## Flagged assumptions

- **Names (Claude's discretion, D-03):** the record `LEAF_VERDICTS` (beside `LEAF_NEEDS`), the test `isHeld` (beside
  the `holds…` family and `Verdict`'s `Held`), outcome types `BinaryOutcome`, `RelationshipOutcome`, `ActedOutcome`,
  `SignatureOutcome`, `LeafOutcome`. D-03's acted `Opposite` kind, which carries the answer that came back, is two
  literals, one per answer: `Performed` (Acted came back to a HasNotActed leaf) and `NotPerformed` (NotActed came back to
  a HasActed leaf). They are the words the walk's sentences already use, and they keep `"Acted"`/`"NotActed"` out of
  the walk entirely.
- **`holdsRole`/`holdsPermission`/`holdsAction` stay as named exports the rows call** (discretion): their unit tests
  and BEH-QD-589 stay, and Walk and Predicate import only `LEAF_VERDICTS` and `isHeld` for verdicts.
- **A row takes the inputs its verdict reads.** The record is keyed by the leaf's tag, so the tag picks the row (D-02's
  "maps the leaf and its answer"); rows that read the leaf's fields take the leaf (role, permission, action, the two
  attribute rows), and rows that read only the port's answer take only the answer (acted, relationship, custom,
  signature). An unused leaf parameter would be dead weight under `noUnusedParameters`.
- **`isHeld` restates `=== "Held"` for the wider outcome union**; it cannot call `holds`, whose parameter is `Verdict`.
  The unit test pins the two equal over the five `Verdict`s.
- **The RED test is its own commit**, before the src commit, as Phase 53's RED tests were; at that commit the core suite
  and `pnpm typecheck` fail on the missing export, by design.
- **Comments may cite ADR-QD-167 and ARCH-101** before 54-06 writes the ADR; `check:pr` runs only after 54-07.
