  `idOf(stored)` reads `JSON.parse(text).record.evaluationId` on success and `failure.evaluationId` on refusal, the same
  on both sides of the change.

  any `at` (`DecisionLog.test.ts:50` does exactly this). `collectingLogger` is in `packages/core/test/helpers.ts`
  (`ShippedSinkReports.test.ts` shows its use).

  failed` and retains nothing; after D-01 and D-12 nothing reads the getter.
