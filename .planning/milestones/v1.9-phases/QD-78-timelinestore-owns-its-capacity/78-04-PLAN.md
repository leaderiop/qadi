<objective>
Give the store its own capacity and let the feed hold that one store, as one compile-green change (TRSZ-01, TRSZ-02,
TRSZ-03, WX-04, WX-06, WX-07). **D-01:** `TimelineStore.resize(capacity): boolean`, validate first, the same effective
capacity a no-op answering `false`, otherwise empty at the new bound, release the pause, `publish` once and answer
`true`. **D-04:** a resize while paused releases the pause; the same capacity while paused keeps it; a refused capacity
keeps it. **D-12:** `clear` empties at the capacity the store holds now. **D-05:** the feed holds one store built at

capacity changes nothing in `configure`, through `effectiveCapacity`, which 78-05's dock calls too. **D-07:**
`idleTimeline.resize` answers `false`.

store nothing feeds, and a rejected input cannot leave the feed with inputs it never took. The store's own rules are
pinned one by one in 78-05; this plan proves the path end to end.

timeline resize`.
