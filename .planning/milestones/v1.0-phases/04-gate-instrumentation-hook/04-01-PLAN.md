<objective>
Make gate instrumentation one hook over a writer the provider resolves once (ARCH-49).

`QadiProvider` calls `gateWriterFor(gates)` once and hands the result, `undefined` unless `instrument` is on, to every gate through an internal context that `GateWriter.ts` declares (D-01). `GateWriter.ts` gains `useGates(kind, questions)`. It owns the grouped read, the per-entry atom lookup, `GateIdentity` construction, the marker rule and registration (D-02). `useGate` becomes a thin caller of it. `useDecisionSuspense` suspends on the atom the hook opened instead of opening a second one (D-04). The grouped hooks (`useQuestions`, `usePolicies`) move onto `useGates` in plan 04-02.

Purpose: GATE-01, GATE-02, GATE-03 (its `useDecisionSuspense` half) and GATE-05. Today the writer is looked up in three places by identical text, identity construction is written twice, and the marker rule sits apart from the hook every surface calls.
