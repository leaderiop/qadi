<objective>
Move the JSON encode-refusal vocabulary out of `SinkWire.ts` into a new leaf, `JsonRefusal.ts`, next to `JsonHazard.ts`, so that the port wire stops depending on the sink wire's module (SINK-02, SINK-03, D-04, D-05, D-06). `SinkWire.ts` keeps the record wire's own words: `SinkRecordTag`, `WireVersion`, `WIRE_VERSIONS` and `DecodeRefusal`.

Purpose: ARCH-31 extracted the JSON walk (`JsonHazard.ts`) so that a port question refuses exactly what a sink record refuses. The walk and `PortWireEncode.ts` still import their refusal words from `SinkWire.ts`, so `AttributeResolver.ts`, `CustomPredicate.ts`, `PortWireEncode.ts` and `JsonHazard.ts` all depend on the sink wire for words about JSON, not about records. After this plan both wires read one JSON leaf.

Output: `JsonRefusal.ts` (new); `SinkWire.ts` trimmed; every importer retargeted; the barrel and the house-style exemption updated. No export is added or removed.

Leaf decision (Claude's discretion, D-04): a separate `JsonRefusal.ts`, not folded into `JsonHazard.ts`. `JsonHazard.ts` is package-private and out of the barrel (ADR-QD-099), and the moved names are public: `spec/overview.md`'s Errors section names every one, and the barrel re-exports `SinkWire.ts` wholesale today. Folding them in would mean either a named export in core's pure `export *` barrel, which takes the file out of the `barrel-order` check, or putting `wireHazard`/`toJson` on the public surface. Plan 09-03's ADR-QD-116 records this choice.
</objective>
