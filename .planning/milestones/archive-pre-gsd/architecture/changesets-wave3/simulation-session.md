The simulator's state is a headless session, and its form is a round-tripping codec. New: `makeSimulationSession` and the `SimulationSession` store (with `resolveSource`, `sourceOptions` and the snapshot types), the `SimulationForm` codecs (`attributeCodec`, `edgeCodec`, `eventCodec`, `signatureCodec`, `roleCodec`, `permissionCodec` and their list helpers), and, from `@qadi/devtools/react`, `useSimulationSession`, `useOwnedSimulationSession` and `useSimulationSnapshot`; `SimulatorProps` gains an optional `session`. Fixed: a chosen `Live` source with no `ports` (or `Snapshot` with no capture) is refused as a typed state instead of answering from fixtures; a run left behind by a policy change or a re-seed is dropped instead of landing under another policy or another row's baseline; the "what if" sweep runs the evaluations the cost line states (the pairs toggle was ignored); fixture edges and events keep their subject, a relation containing a colon is not split, and a string value that reads as a number is shown quoted; signatures are editable in the Fixtures card; and the dock keeps the simulator's form, capture and result across tab switches. A capture is dropped when the host's `ports` change.
