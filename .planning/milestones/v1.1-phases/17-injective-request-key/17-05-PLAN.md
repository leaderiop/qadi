<objective>
Implement the human's D-04 answer (KEY-04): what a devtools Snapshot replay answers for a request its capture never saw, on every port, with one test per port proving it under the leaf and under `not(leaf)`.

Purpose: a replay that answers `none` on a miss can show an allow under `not(...)` that the real store would not give (INV-QD-007 fail-open, PROJECT.md Known Issues). Plan 17-01 recorded which ports close that; this plan makes the replay do it and pins it per port. After plans 17-03 and 17-04, a "miss" is exactly "no captured answer under an Equal key", so the policy is now about questions genuinely not asked, not about collisions.

Output: `Capture.ts`'s miss policy per the recorded outcome, and a per-port table test.
</objective>
