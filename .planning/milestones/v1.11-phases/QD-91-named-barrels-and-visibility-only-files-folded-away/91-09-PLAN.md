<objective>
Replace the three `export *` barrels with generated named barrels, one commit per barrel, the public surface unchanged
(D-11, D-11a, D-12, D-13, D-14, D-15, BARL-01, BARL-02).

naming the exports first is what lets each later fold keep its other names package-private.
