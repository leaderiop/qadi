<objective>
Move the reading into the feed's one snapshot and hand the engine a sink: delete the store, its factory and the `marking`
wrapper, keeping the guard order and the publish-then-fork shape (D-08 to D-10, D-19, D-21, D-18 step 7).

Purpose: DIAG-02. The feed held two copies of the reading, the store's and the published snapshot's, kept in step by a
wrapper that imitated a store. With the fold a value function, the snapshot is the reading, and the engine writes through
a one-member sink like `runSource`'s, so nothing can disagree.

Output: `refactor(83-07): the feed folds each sample into its one snapshot through a sink` (source, the engine tests'
harness, the type pins, the restated doc comments, the evidence) and `test(83-07): the root does not export
DiagnosticsSink`.
