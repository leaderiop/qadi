- **Context**: what a hide of an `<Activity>` and a StrictMode double effect do to a provider's effects; the defect (a
  hoisted provider revealed after a hide longer than a macrotask met a disposed registry, so the consumers' subscriptions
  and the provider's subject effect threw `registry is disposed`; a re-render while hidden silently built a second
  registry that re-applied seeds); the commit order, quoted from `82-RED-EVIDENCE.md`'s observed lines; and that React
  gives no sign at cleanup time whether a subtree is hidden or gone.
- **Decision**: one registry per provider, built in render with the subject and `initialValues` seeded, kept for the
  provider's life; `RegistryLease.ts` (`makeRegistryLease`, package-private) decides when it is recycled: a release
  deferred one macrotask, cancelled by a hold, its state a closed union of three; the provider only holds and releases;
  the recycle is `registry.reset()` then the latest committed subject written back, read from a ref written in an
  effect; neither `initialValues` nor a hydration seed is written back; the lease never touches the sweep,
  `scheduleTask` or `defaultIdleTTL` (ADR-QD-148, CCR-QD-150); its matchers are built once per lease, not at module
  scope, because a module-scope matcher's arms are mutants `ignoreStatic` leaves unjudged.
- **Alternatives considered**, each with its reason: rebuilding in an effect or in state (too late: children's reveal
  effects run first); the scoped-hook shape (renders nothing on the server pass, losing SSR and the first-render subject);
  a registry proxy (forwards every member and hides identity); disposing only on a true unmount (React gives no
  hidden-or-gone signal); never releasing (it retains the subject, the seed grants, the runtime's layer atom and host
  `initialValues` atoms past the provider's life; idle removal already releases an unsubscribed runtime layer, so the
  reason is retention, not leaked fibers); `dispose()` in place of `reset()` (terminal, so a straggler or a reveal
  throws); re-applying seeds after a recycle (a seed is not an authority, ADR-QD-039, and the client's answer takes
  precedence, ADR-QD-017).
- **Consequences**: after a long hide gates render `Pending`, then the client's own answer; the change to a long hide
  with a re-render (it painted the superseded allow and reported a second hydration mismatch; it now renders `Pending`
  and reports once); a seed with no subject, or with a resolver that never answers, stays `Pending` after a long hide,
  fail-closed; `initialValues` seed construction only and a host atom returns to its default; a host's own
  `registry.subscribe` or `mount` held across a hide is detached, while this package's hooks have unsubscribed before
  the recycle runs; the accepted trade (a straggling read after unmount recreates a node that holds nothing alive and is
  removed a macrotask later, measured, no leak); `AtomsReplaced` is unchanged; the sweep bound holds across a long hide.
