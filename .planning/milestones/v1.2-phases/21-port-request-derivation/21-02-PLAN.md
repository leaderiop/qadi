<objective>
Make a port's wire question exhaustive by type (D-01, PREQ-04, PREQ-05): `toQuestion` must name every member of the question,
writing an absent one as `undefined`, and `portWire` drops those before the strict schema encode. Every conditional spread in
the five `toQuestion` bodies goes, and the wire bytes do not change.

Purpose: a conditional spread that is left out compiles today and silently drops a field from the wire (and, after 21-03, from
the key). With `QuestionOf<Q>` the omission is TS2741.

Output: `PortWireEncode.ts` with `QuestionOf` and `presentMembers`, five simpler `toQuestion` bodies, the question half of
`PortRequest.tst.ts` green.
</objective>
