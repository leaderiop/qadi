  readonly entry: TimelineEntry; readonly replayed: ReplayedPolicy | undefined }` (the row and, while its policy is the

  ReadonlyArray<PolicySighting>; readonly held: PolicySighting | undefined; readonly seed: SeedRow | undefined }`;
  `Seeding { readonly choice: SeededChoice; readonly input: SimulationInput | undefined }`. `PolicyChoice`,
  `PolicyMenuOption` and `PolicyMenu` move verbatim from `SimulationSession.ts` with their doc comments; the tie

- `seedFrom(choice, entry)`: one `replayInput(entry)` when `entry` is defined; `seed` is `undefined` for no entry, else
  `{ entry, replayed }` with `replayed` the `Replayable`'s `{ policy, unseeded }` or `undefined`; `held` is
  `pinUnlessReplayed(choice.sightings, choice.held, seed)`; `input` is the `Replayable`'s `input` or `undefined`.

  sightings, held: pinUnlessReplayed(sightings, choice.held, choice.seed) }`.

  when `same` and `choice.seed?.replayed === undefined`; else `{ ...choice, held: same ? choice.held :
  sightingFor(choice.sightings, policy), seed: choice.seed === undefined ? undefined : { entry: choice.seed.entry,
  replayed: undefined } }`.

  `resolveHeld(choice.sightings, choice.held, sameSighting)`. `policyOf(choice)`: the `Seeded` arm's policy, else
  `heldValue(picked)?.policy`. `seedOf(choice)`: `undefined` for no seed, `seed.entry` while replayed, `undefined`
  for a `TimelineDecision` row, else `seed.entry`. `seededFrom(choice)`: `choice.seed?.entry`.
