**Settle rule.** After an unmount two or more `Effect.yieldNow` are needed (the registry's removal task, then the sweep fiber); the harness uses 20. `beforeEach` also settles before it resets the counters, because a sweep pending from the previous test's cleanup otherwise lands in the next test's count (research Pitfall 5; it produced a false release-only failure in E1b before the fix). `TestClock` is not used: a fiber from `Effect.runFork` does not see it.

**Sweeps are counted, not signals.** A recompute's momentary zero and repeated signals coalesce into one fiber per macrotask, so (d) counts `book.sweep` calls per pressure episode.

### Decisions

**The admission trigger: kept.** Rule (D-05, research open question 1): kept when release-plus-admission passes every criterion, dropped only when it fails a criterion release-only passes, the e2e being the tiebreak. Release-plus-admission passes (a) to (f) and the D-03 checks and its e2e is `30 passed (8.0s)` against the base `30 passed (7.2s)`; release-only fails (b), (d) and (e) as written and one more existing test. The failure is the same single fact each time: release-only does not bound a question opened and never read (BEH-QD-198 requires that bound), and it passes only once those questions are mounted and released. There is no criterion release-only passes that release-plus-admission fails, so the e2e tiebreak was not needed and the release-only e2e was not run. Admission was safe on the render path and in the dispose-reread window: R1 swept 0 synchronously and W1 swept 0 synchronously.

**The release guard `entry.inBook`: kept.** O1 builds the orphan: an entry swept, a newer entry opened and mounted for the same key, the old atom's reader mounted (counted as a reader, not re-admitted) and then released, over an all-live book at bound 1. With the guard: signals 0, sweeps 0, `asked()` 3. Without it (`entry.liveCount === 0 && order.length > capacity`): signals 1, sweeps 1, `asked()` 3, a sweep that evicts nothing. The guard changes nothing but that spurious signal, so it earns its place by removing it.

**The cold-count guard (Pitfall 4): not added.** C1 mounts 200 live questions one per turn over a bound of 5, so every admission is over capacity and nothing can be evicted: 195 signals and 195 sweeps (one per turn, each an O(n) scan), `asked()` 200, 82 ms for the whole loop including the yields. The scan is coalesced to one per turn and the cost is not visible: the e2e is `30 passed (8.0s)` with the scan live. A count of cold entries would add a second counter to maintain at two places for no measured gain.

**The late commit (Pitfall 1): re-admission recovers it.** L1 (the research's P9 case, run on the real tree at bound 1 with one live entry): right after a render-phase `registry.get` of a new question `asked()` is 2; after one turn with no subscription it is 1 (the registry released the uncommitted node and the sweep evicted the question); after the late subscribe re-reads it, `asked()` is 2 again (`live()` re-admitted it) and stays 2 after a settle; sweeps 2. The cost is a transient absence from `asked()` at the bound only. H1 shows the hydration side: three seeds over a bound of 2 written before any mount and all mounted in one synchronous span all survive (`asked()` 3, all three `isSeeded`, 1 sweep that evicted nothing); a seed nobody reads is forgotten at the first sweep, as it was when the provider's first sweep ran, one macrotask after the pressure rather than after the children rendered. The e2e (real Suspense, real browser, where `yieldNow` is a `setTimeout`) is `30 passed (8.0s)`, so no dropped render appeared.

**Result for candidate 1.** release-plus-admission passes every criterion (a) to (f) and the D-03 exclusions, with the e2e at `30 passed (8.0s)`; release-only fails (b), (d) and (e) as written and is not carried further. The tree is back at the base: `git status --porcelain -- packages` prints nothing and `pnpm vitest run --project react` is `Tests  442 passed (442)`.
