## Flagged assumptions

- **D-10's compile-error promise holds for `entry` rows only.** D-10 says a new `payload` or `entry` row is a compile
  error in react's dispatchers, and also that those dispatchers stay untouched. Probed: `bucketOf` is total over
  `EntryDropReason`, so an entry row is TS2741 there; nothing in react is total over `PayloadRefusalReason` (the payload
  map goes from the decoder's refusal tags to a reason), so a payload row compiles, reaches the reporters with its
  meaning from the row, and is emitted by nothing until a refusal that produces it is written. Making it a compile
  error would mean adding a reason-keyed construct to `HydrationEngine.ts`, which D-10 and the Phase 42 edge forbid.
  This plan keeps the dispatchers untouched (the explicit half of D-10), records the payload result as found, and
  39-04 states it in ADR-QD-152's consequences. Surfaced to the orchestrator for the user.

  code-only scan and the derived declarations. The core half is 39-02's.

  reporters", recorded here; the unchanged-metric half is 39-02's block check and 39-05's D-17 checks.
