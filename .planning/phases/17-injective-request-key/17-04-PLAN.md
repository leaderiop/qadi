<objective>
Handle the key's `Result` at every consumer (KEY-01, KEY-03), against the tree as Phases 11 and 13 left it: a refused request fails closed with the port's own typed error, a capture never changes a live result, and no `Result` is ever used as a map key.

This plan finds every such consumer on the current tree, not the 1721a42 one, and closes each explicitly.

Output: three consumer changes, their tests, an inventory and a map-key gate, and the repo-wide typecheck green again.
</objective>
