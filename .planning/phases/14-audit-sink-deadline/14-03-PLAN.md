<objective>
Bound every audit store call by a deadline the sink owns, and count a deadline as a failed attempt.

Purpose: AUD-01 to AUD-03. `AuditDecisionSinkLive` today waits on `trailPort.write` / `stagingPort.stage` / `stagingPort.commit` for as long as they take. A hung store keeps `evaluate` waiting forever and keeps the breaker `Closed`, so the one mechanism meant to shed a sick store never fires. The core value — "an authorization decision is never wrong and never silent" — needs the decision to come back.
Output: the deadline option on `AuditDecisionSinkLive`, applied to all three calls; 14-01's RED file turned GREEN on scripted doubles, plus boundary tests.
