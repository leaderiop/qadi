<objective>
Give the shipped audit doubles a per-call script (D-05): each call to `write`, `stage` or `commit` is answered by a reply from a closed union — Ok, Fail, Die, (Interrupt), Never, Latch — interpreted once, in one module both doubles share.

Purpose: AUD-04's first half. A hung store (`Never`), a store the test can watch arrive and hang (`Latch`), a defecting adapter (`Die`) and a self-interrupting one (`Interrupt`) become public, one-line scripts instead of hand-written layers. This is what lets 14-03 express the deadline tests and 14-04 delete the 11 hand layers and `driveExit`'s mode table.
Output: `AuditPortReply.ts` (new, exported), both doubles scripted, their tests extended, a type-test file, and `spec/overview.md` rows.
</objective>
