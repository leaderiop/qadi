answering `false`, otherwise empty at the new bound, release the pause, `publish` once and answer `true`. **D-04:** a

capacity changes nothing in `configure`. **D-07:** `idleTimeline.resize` answers `false`.

store nothing feeds, and a rejected input cannot leave the feed with inputs it never took.

resize rules of the timeline store`.
