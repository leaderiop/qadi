## What the timeout does and does not do

`execFileSync` kills the child it started at `CHILD_TIMEOUT_MS`. The TypeScript server the child started may outlive a killed child; nothing here kills it. The planted hang shows the gate no longer waits on it (the hook's hang ended at 3000 ms), not that no server process is left behind.
