## The behaviours

| Id | Title | REQUIREMENT (MUST statements) | Pinned by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BEH-QD-696 | A derived store recomputes exactly when a field of its key changes identity | A derived store MUST compute once when built and notify no listener; on each parent notification and each `refresh` it MUST read its key and recompute exactly when some own field of either key differs by identity or is present on one side only; a key type with an optional field, an empty parent list and a compute reading a field outside its key MUST be compile errors | `ExternalStore.test.ts` rows 1 to 7 and 11; `ExternalStore.tst.ts` |
| BEH-QD-697 | A derived store notifies once per change, before the listeners added after it | On a key change it MUST compute, then commit the key, then set the value: a throwing compute MUST commit nothing, a compute returning the held reference MUST notify nothing, a refresh from inside its own listener MUST notify once; it MUST be subscribed to its parents in the order listed when built; its listeners MUST follow BEH-QD-656, and what they throw MUST reach the parent's loop as one value | rows 8 to 10 and 12 to 14 |
| BEH-QD-698 | A derived store does nothing after its scope closes | After the scope that built it closes, it MUST read no key, call no compute, release every parent, ignore `refresh` and keep its last snapshot | row 15 |
| BEH-QD-699 | The dock model's three computed stores are derived stores | The dock model's shown reading (key: the feed's snapshot and the supplied reading), `questions` (key: `JoinKey`) and `services` (key: `ServicesInput`) MUST be derived stores, built in that order; `configure` MUST refresh the shown reading after configuring both feeds; a model whose scope closed MUST merge no reading a later `configure` hands it | the three per-store tests, the closed-model regression test, `DockModel.characterisation.test.ts` |
| BEH-QD-700 | The dock model's stores are listed once | The dock model's stores MUST be exactly the members of `DockModel` that have `subscribe` and `getSnapshot`: `timeline`, `dock`, `explorer`, `roles`, `simulation`, `diagnostics`, `timelineStatus`, `questions` and `services`; `sightings` is a thunk and not a store | the store-list test |

Each section's prose is two to four present-tense sentences on why (BEH-QD-697: the derived store is a store built on
`makeExternalStore`, its refresh one listener in each parent's set, so a host listener that throws silences neither
the store's other listeners nor the parent's; BEH-QD-699: the key reads the model's inputs after `configure` assigns
them, so one `configure` publishes one join; BEH-QD-700: this section is the one place the stores are named and
counted, and the test fails on any store added without it; earlier records counted the policy rail among the stores,
so they call the diagnostics the seventh store and the timeline status the eighth, and those ordinals stay as written
where they appear). BEH-QD-696 to BEH-QD-698 cite INV-QD-389; BEH-QD-699 and BEH-QD-700 cite INV-QD-390. Every
pinned-by names the test file paths and the exact titles.

## The invariants

- INV-QD-389, statement as D-15 words it. Source: `packages/devtools/src/model/ExternalStore.ts` (`makeDerivedStore`,
  `sameFields`). Implication: no store built on it can miss a key field or commit a key its compute failed on.
  Enforcement: `packages/devtools/test/model/ExternalStore.test.ts` (`describe("makeDerivedStore")`) and
  `packages/devtools/test/model/ExternalStore.tst.ts` (the optional field, the empty parent list, the field outside the
  key, each a compile error); a compute that reads state outside its key is checked by neither. Related: ADR-QD-185,
  INV-QD-373, BEH-QD-696 to BEH-QD-698.
- INV-QD-390, statement as D-15 means it, worded "their count is stated once, in BEH-QD-700" (never the phrase
  "store count", which 72-08's close sweep admits only in BEH-QD-700, ADRs, CCR rows and blockquotes; the same holds
  for its traceability row). Source: `packages/devtools/src/model/DockModel.ts` (the three
  `makeDerivedStore` calls). Implication: a store added to the model is a test failure until BEH-QD-700 and the test
  list it. Enforcement: the store-list test in `packages/devtools/test/model/DockModel.test.ts` checks the count; that
  `DockModel.ts` keeps no last key and no closed state of its own is checked by no test or gate: it follows from the
  three stores being derived stores, which the per-store tests, the closed-model regression test and
  `DockModel.characterisation.test.ts` exercise. Related: ADR-QD-185, INV-QD-357, INV-QD-389, BEH-QD-699, BEH-QD-700.
